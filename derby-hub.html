How to read sharp money moves

Steam means money is flooding in, compressing the price. This can be sharp (professional) money acting on information, or heavy public betting on a recognizable name. Look for horses shortening >30% from morning line — those are the ones the market is most confident in.

Drifting means money is going away from a horse. Either the public is fading them (creating value) or sharp money is betting against them. A drifting favorite at post time is a classic overlay signal for exotics.

Key rule: In pari-mutuel racing, the final odds at gate opening are all that matter — not the morning line. Watch the tote board in the final 5 minutes before each race. The biggest moves happen in the last 2 minutes when large bets hit the pool simultaneously.
